Youhua PT1570-H10-CU
====================

The Youhua PT1570-H10-CU is the China Unicom variant of the PT1570-H10.
It uses the Realtek RTL9617C SoC with one 2.5-Gigabit port, three gigabit
ports and two PCIe WiFi 6 cards.

It is a separate target from ``pt1570-h10-ct`` on purpose.  Although the
FIP geometry and UUIDs are shared, the two products ship different stock
boot records (per-unit GPT GUID, BL2 and BL31), a different front-panel
lamp map, and slightly different firmware DTS PHY wiring.  Flashing a
CT-built boot image onto a CU unit overwrites those vendor regions and
the unit will not boot.

Build target
------------

.. code-block:: bash

   make youhua-pt1570-h10-cu-2p5g_defconfig

The default device tree is:

.. code-block:: none

   rtl9617c-youhua-pt1570-h10-cu-2p5g

Board files
-----------

The board implementation is under ``board/youhua/pt1570-h10-cu/``.
``desc-2p5g.c`` lists the eleven U-Boot-drivable status lamps in physical
front-panel order; the 5G / 2.4G WiFi lamps are driven by the PCIe cards and
the 2.5G jack lamp by the external PHY, so those positions are skipped.

Release image
-------------

``tools/release/boards/youhua-pt1570-h10-cu-2p5g.json`` records the geometry,
FIP UUIDs and the digest of the CU stock boot record.  The stock binary
itself is **not** included in this tree: it is device-specific and must be
backed up from the target unit (``mtd0``, 2 MiB) and supplied to
``tools/release/pack_boot.py`` with ``--stock`` at build time.  ``pack_boot.py``
keeps GPT / BL2 / BL31 byte-identical to that stock and replaces only BL33.
